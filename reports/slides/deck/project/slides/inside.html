Dense builds the relation layer by layer; SUT stops after one step

Dense uses all 6 layers

Region bits climb from 0.50 to 0.75. The edit reaches <cls> at layers 3–4 (R 0.52 → 0.94).

SUT plateaus at step 1

Region probe stuck at 0.53 (3× budget: 0.69 by step 3). Information arrives early but is not transformed.

MoM never leaves chance

Label 0.16 and region 0.50 at every step, the same as a random-weight model.

Left and middle panels: balanced accuracy of linear probes on the state after each step, for the 7-way label and the four region bits. Chance is 1/7 and 0.5. Right panel: activation patching, the fraction of the clean–corrupt logit difference recovered by patching only the state at that step. Dense: the region bits become linearly readable only from layer 2 on and keep improving to layer 6 (0.75). Patching shows the edited token's information stays at its own position through layer 2 and moves into at layers 3 and 4. So the dense model computes the relation over its whole depth. SUT with the released settings: the probes jump a little at step 1 and then stay flat (region 0.53, label 0.22). Patching shows information does reach early, but the states no longer change in a useful way. The 3x-budget SUT gets further (0.69 by step 3) and then also plateaus. SUT fixed-depth (no halting) keeps improving to step 6 but at a lower level than Dense. MoM and its dense donor: probes stay at chance at every step, indistinguishable from the random-init control. Their clause-token states even carry less truth-table information than the raw embeddings. Truth-table probes (not shown): clause meanings are about as decodable from random features (0.66) as from any trained model (0.66 to 0.71), so no model computes explicit truth tables. SAE: the best single SAE feature correlates with the region bits at |r| = 0.52 on average in Dense, 0.23 in SUT, 0.32 in SUT 3x, 0.16 in MoM.
