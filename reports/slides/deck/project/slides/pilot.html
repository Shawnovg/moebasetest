Test accuracy, mean over seeds. Solid bars: held-out pairs of the training lengths. Light bars: longer formulas, 7 to 12 operators, never seen in training. The dotted line is the majority class. Dense is best at 0.736. Experts in a dense model (Switch, MoA) cost a little. Sharing one block (UT) costs more. SUT with the released code reaches only 0.551, and fixing the MIM sign helps a little (0.574). Training SUT three times longer brings it to 0.691, still below Dense at one third of its budget. Both MoM and its dense donor stay at the majority class. On longer formulas everything is close to chance; only Dense keeps a clear margin (0.571). These results raise the question for the rest of the talk: what do SUT and MoM compute differently, layer by layer, and which of their components are responsible?
