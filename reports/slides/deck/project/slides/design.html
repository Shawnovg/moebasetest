MoM assembly

10 pilot checkpoints (seed 0), d = 128, 6 block applications, read out at <cls>. Schematic redrawn after Shen et al. 2023 (SUT, Fig. 1, 3) and Gong et al. 2024 (MoM, Fig. 1).

The task: two propositional clauses over six variables a to f. Each clause has a truth set over the 64 possible worlds. The label is the set relation between the two truth sets: entailment either way, equivalence, negation, alternation, cover, or independence. Because the generator rejects tautologies and contradictions, the label is a fixed function of four region bits: whether A and B overlap, whether A has worlds outside B, B outside A, and whether some world satisfies neither. That is what we probe for later. Splits: train on formulas with at most 6 operators, test on held-out formulas of the same lengths and on longer ones with 7 to 12 operators. The majority class is independence at 54%. Models: the ten pilot checkpoints at seed 0. Dense is six untied post-LN blocks. UT is one shared block applied six times. Switch and MoA put experts into the FFN or into attention of a dense model. SUT is one shared block whose attention query/output projections and FFN are mixtures of experts, with per-token stick-breaking halting and the MIM auxiliary loss; we look at the released MIM sign, the paper sign, a fixed-depth version and a 3x budget run. MoM is converted from a GPT-2-style dense donor: a bottom layer, a 4-step block where a GRU router chooses one attention and one FFN module per token (or SKIP), and an anchor layer. The released SUT code computes H(E|X) + H(E) instead of the paper's H(E|X) − H(E); this was verified earlier.
