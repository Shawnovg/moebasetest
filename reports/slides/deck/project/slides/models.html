Schematic redrawn after Shen et al. 2023 (SUT, Fig. 1, 3) and Gong et al. 2024 (MoM, Fig. 1).

All models share width 128, six block applications and the same input format, so differences come from the structure. Dense: six separate post-LN blocks. UT: one dense block applied six times. Switch: experts in the FFN of each layer, top-1, Switch load-balancing loss. MoA: attention heads as experts, 4 of 8 per token, with SUT's MIM loss. SUT (Shen et al. 2023): one shared block whose attention query and output projections and FFN are mixtures of experts (4 experts, top-2), plus stick-breaking halting: each token stops once its probability of not having halted falls below 1 − 0.999, and the output is the halting-weighted mix of the step outputs. The MIM auxiliary loss is meant to make expert use balanced overall but confident per token; the released code adds the marginal entropy instead of subtracting it. MoM (Gong et al. 2024): converted from a trained dense donor. A bottom layer and an anchor layer stay dense; the middle four layers become pools of 4 attention and 4 FFN modules, and a GRU router assembles one attention and one FFN module per token at each of 4 steps, or skips.
